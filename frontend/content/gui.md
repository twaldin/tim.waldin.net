## projects

### [easl](/projects/easl)

a native mac app for coding agents. real terminals, code tiles, call graphs, browsers, notes, and html pages share one infinite board. hyper-click a shape, page element, or note to send it with your next prompt. free and MIT, for macOS 14 or later on apple silicon.

[easl.sh](https://easl.sh) · [source](https://github.com/twaldin/easl) · [why i built it](/blog/2026-10-04-easl)
